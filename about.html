Our story

From advising institutions to building their tools

Leowa Associates Limited is a management consulting firm registered in Kenya, working across the East African region on institutional strengthening, ICT systems and public-sector reform. Our consultants combine international training with strong local insight.

After years spent inside schools and public institutions helping them fix broken processes on paper and in spreadsheets, we built SHUP School Management System to put that same fix directly into schools' hands — without needing to hire a consultant every time.
