What's inside

Everything your school needs, in one place

Six modules that replace the registers, ledgers and WhatsApp groups schools juggle today.

01

Student Information Management

Centralise admissions, profiles, class allocation and academic history in one secure record per learner.

02

Academic & Examination Management

Manage subjects, timetables, assignments, exams and grading — with report generation teachers can trust.

03

Finance & Fee Management

Fee invoicing, payment tracking and receipts, with clear financial reporting for the bursar's office.

04

Communication & Notifications

Reach parents and staff instantly with SMS, email and in-app announcements and reminders.

05

Reports & Analytics Dashboard

Enrollment, attendance, finance and performance insights that support real planning decisions.

06

Parent & Student Portal

Real-time access to attendance, grades, announcements and schedules — from any phone.
