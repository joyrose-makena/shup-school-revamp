FAQ

Common questions

How long does onboarding take?

Most schools are fully set up within a week — student and staff records can be imported from existing spreadsheets.

Can parents access it without a smartphone?

Yes — the parent portal works on any browser, and SMS notifications reach parents without data or a smartphone.

Is our data secure?

Student and financial records are access-controlled by role, so only authorised staff can view or edit sensitive information.

Do you offer training for staff?

Yes — every onboarding includes hands-on training for teachers, bursars and administrators, drawing on our consulting background.
